TICAS Rural Reconnect Map (beta)

This is a test version of a data product by The Institute for College Access & Success (TICAS) presented in partnership with MDRC.

To use the map, use the control panel on the right to make adjustments to the map. Click on a commuting zone to view county-level data. Clicking on a commuter zone or county displays demographic and education data below the map. On mobile, click the button in the top right corner to display the control panel.

Use the links in the navigation bar above to learn more about TICAS and the rural reconnect initiative.

Data downloads are not currently available, but will be made available after official release.

About this project

The Rural Reconnect Initiative is a partnership led by The Institute for College Access and Success (TICAS) in partnership with MDRC. In collaboration with institutions, the initative aims to advance college completion and economic mobility for Black and Latino students in rural communities across the South and Southwest.
