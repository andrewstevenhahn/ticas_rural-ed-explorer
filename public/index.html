About this project

The Rural Reconnect Initiative is a partnership led by The Institute for College Access and Success (TICAS) in partnership with MDRC. In collaboration with institutions, the initative aims to advance college completion and economic mobility for Black and Latino students in rural communities across the South and Southwest.
